Brideio – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

1. Data Collected

User-uploaded photos, text inputs, and optional information may be processed.

Device info, usage analytics, crash reports, and anonymized IP data may be collected automatically.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • Performing AI-based media processing
  • Improving service performance and user experience
  • Detecting and resolving technical issues
  • Meeting legal obligations

3. Third-Party AI Services

AI operations in Brideio are performed via third-party services. Submitted content is only used temporarily for processing and is not stored permanently.

APIs used may retain data for technical/log purposes for a maximum of 60 days; data is not used for model training or advertising.

4. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. To request deletion of content: brideio.app@gmail.com

5. Data Security and Retention

Uploaded content is stored on cloud servers only for the duration of the requested processing, and is retained for technical/log purposes for a maximum of 60 days. Content is not permanently stored on application servers.

6. Analytics and Telemetry

Device info, usage analytics, and other technical data are collected for service improvement, error reporting, and performance monitoring.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when necessary and changes will be announced in the app.
